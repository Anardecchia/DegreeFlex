Healthcare Programs at HCI College

Real Training for Real Healthcare Careers

Every HCI College program is built around hands-on clinical experience, seasoned healthcare instructors, and career outcomes. Whether you're starting fresh or advancing an existing healthcare career, HCI has a program designed for your goals — at two convenient South Florida campuses.

Medical Assisting
Diploma Program · 10 Months

A 36-credit-hour diploma program covering both clinical and administrative skills for entry-level employment as a Medical Assistant. Includes hybrid delivery — two days online, two days on campus per week.

  • Hybrid delivery — online & on-campus
  • CCMA exam taken on campus at graduation
  • Phlebotomy & EKG Technician exam eligibility
  • Tablet, textbooks & uniforms included

Practical Nursing
Diploma Program

Combines theoretical instruction with hands-on clinical experience and simulation in medical-surgical nursing, pharmacology, maternal-newborn care, and more. Prepares students to sit for the NCLEX-PN exam.

  • Clinical simulation & real-world placements
  • Med-surg, pharmacology, OB, pediatrics
  • NCLEX-PN exam prep included
  • Dedicated clinical faculty support

Associate Degree in Nursing
Associate Degree · 2 Years · ACEN Accredited

A 72-credit-hour associate degree program combining general education online courses with on-campus nursing lecture, labs, and clinical rotations. Fort Lauderdale campus recently granted ACEN initial accreditation.

  • ACEN-accredited at Fort Lauderdale campus

  • NCLEX-RN exam preparation

Why HCI College

Caregivers Train Here. South Florida Hires Here.

HCI College has been preparing South Florida healthcare workers for decades. The mission has never changed: practical, career-focused education that connects directly to the healthcare workforce — led by instructors who've worked in the field.

Clinical labs, simulation equipment, and real-world externship placements mean you're building practical skills from your first semester — not reading about them from a textbook.

Hybrid Flexibility

Programs like Medical Assisting offer a hybrid delivery model — two days online, two days on campus — so you can keep working while you train. HCI builds schedules that fit real life.

Career Placement Support

Financial Aid Available

Two Campus Locations

Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach campuses give South Florida students convenient access to quality healthcare training close to home, with advisors at each location ready to support you.

Accreditation & Approvals

Credentials That Employers and Licensing Boards Recognize

HCI College's accreditations and approvals are what Florida employers and nursing licensing boards look for — and what unlock your financial aid eligibility. This isn't a diploma mill; it's a recognized, career-focused institution with decades of healthcare graduates.

ACCSC Accredited

Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) — the nationally recognized accreditor for career and technical institutions offering practical, career-focused education.

ACEN Accredited — Nursing

The Fort Lauderdale campus Associate Degree in Nursing program has been granted initial accreditation from ACEN — the Accreditation Commission for Education in Nursing. Essential for NCLEX eligibility and employer recognition.

Title IV & Financial Aid

Certified by the U.S. Department of Education to participate in Federal Title IV financial aid programs, including Pell Grants and federal student loans. Scholarship programs also available for qualifying students.

Florida-Licensed Institution

Licensed and approved to operate by the State of Florida Commission for Independent Education — confirming program quality, institutional stability, and legitimacy for Florida students and employers.
