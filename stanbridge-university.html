Why Stanbridge University

Built for Licensure. Recognized by Employers.

Stanbridge University was founded with a singular focus: healthcare education that produces exam-ready, career-ready graduates. Every touchpoint — simulation labs, clinical placements, faculty mentorship, licensure prep — is designed to get you to and through your licensure exam and into practice.

#1 LVN Program in California

From 2019 to 2023, Stanbridge's Orange County LVN program was ranked #1 in California by Nursingprocess.org based on first-time NCLEX-PN pass rates, academic quality, and affordability. That result doesn't happen by accident.

High-Fidelity Simulation Labs

Stanbridge's simulation labs replicate real clinical environments — from hospital rooms to therapy settings — giving students the hands-on repetition needed to develop confidence and clinical judgment before entering a live patient care setting.

ACCSC Accredited

Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) and approved to operate by the California Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education (BPPE). Individual programs hold specialty accreditations including ACOTE for occupational therapy.

Blended & Online Flexibility

Most programs combine online general education or lecture content with on-campus labs and clinical rotations — giving you real flexibility without compromising the hands-on training that healthcare careers demand. The MSN-Ed program is fully online.

Student-Centered Support

From online mentorship to practice exams, cumulative review sessions, and career fairs that connect students directly with employers — Stanbridge wraps students in support from enrollment through graduation and into their first role.

Financial Aid Available

Accreditation & Approvals

Credentials That Licensing Boards and Employers Recognize

Stanbridge University's accreditations and regulatory approvals are what California nursing boards, occupational therapy licensing bodies, and healthcare employers look for. Getting licensed in California requires attending an approved program — and Stanbridge is approved.

ACCSC Accredited

Nationally accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC), a U.S. Department of Education-recognized accreditor. All campuses operate under this accreditation, which also unlocks Federal Title IV financial aid eligibility.

BVNPT & BRN Approved

The LVN program is approved by the California Board of Vocational Nursing and Psychiatric Technicians (BVNPT) at all campus locations. The BSN program is approved by the California Board of Registered Nursing (BRN) at Irvine, Alhambra, and Riverside.

ACOTE Accredited — OT Programs

The Occupational Therapy programs (OTA and MSOT) are accredited by the Accreditation Council for Occupational Therapy Education (ACOTE). ACOTE accreditation is required for NBCOT certification eligibility and OT licensure in California.

Title IV & BPPE Approved

Certified by the U.S. Department of Education for Federal Title IV financial aid. All campuses are approved to operate by the California Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education (BPPE), confirming institutional quality and state compliance.

Paying for School

Don't Let Cost Be the Reason You Don't Start

Stanbridge University participates in Federal Title IV financial aid programs, and admissions advisors are dedicated to helping you understand every option available — before you commit to anything. Most students discover more funding options than they expected once they actually sit down and ask.

Federal Financial Aid

Eligible students may qualify for Pell Grants, federal subsidized and unsubsidized loans, and work-study through FAFSA. Stanbridge's financial aid process deadline is 14 days from acceptance — advisors walk you through every step one-on-one, at no charge, before any enrollment commitment.

Private Loans & Payment Options

Students who don't qualify for federal aid or who need additional funding can explore private loan options through lending partners not affiliated with the school. Stanbridge also accepts personal and bank check payments. All loan paperwork must be signed before attending the program.

Scholarship Opportunities

Stanbridge offers institutional scholarship opportunities for qualifying students. Speak with an admissions advisor to learn about available scholarships based on your program of interest, academic background, and financial situation — some can reduce out-of-pocket costs significantly before classes begin.
