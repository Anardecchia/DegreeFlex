Trade Programs

Choose a Program That Moves You Forward

A four-year degree isn't for everyone. AAI offers focused, hands-on training in high-demand trades — programs designed to get you career-ready and working in your field fast.

Automotive Service Technician

Learn to diagnose, repair, and service vehicles with hands-on training in engine repair, transmission, electrical systems, A/C, and performance tuning. Ford ACE partner school with preparation for ASE and EPA 609 certifications.*

13 Months 71.5 Credit Hours Ford ACE Partner
Combination Welding

Build welding skills across SMAW, MIG, TIG, Flux-Core, and Gas Tungsten Arc processes. Hands-on training prepares you for careers in manufacturing, construction, aerospace, automotive, and more. NCCER certification prep included.*

10 Months 50 Credit Hours NCCER Standards
HVAC & Refrigeration

Train on real heating, cooling, and refrigeration systems with hands-on diagnostics. Learn installation, maintenance, electrical components, and EPA-compliant refrigerant handling. Prepares you for EPA Section 608 certification.*

10 Months 50 Credit Hours EPA 608 Prep
Electrical Technician

Learn residential and commercial electrical systems — wiring, circuits, troubleshooting, and code compliance. Hands-on training builds the diagnostic and repair skills employers look for. NCCER certification prep included.*

10 Months 50 Credit Hours NCCER Standards
Construction & Trades Management

Learn to lead construction projects from planning to completion. Covers project management, supervision, scheduling, budgeting, and safety compliance. NCCER Project Management and Supervision curriculum integrated.*

10 Months 50 Credit Hours NCCER Curriculum
Diesel–Heavy Truck Technician

Train to maintain and repair diesel engines, heavy trucks, and large-scale commercial equipment. Hands-on training covers diesel fuel systems, brakes, electrical, and hydraulics for the heavy truck industry.

What Sets AAI Apart

Focused on Your Goals. So Are We.

You're committed to creating the life you want, and AAI's programs can help you get there. Here's what makes our approach different.

Hands-On From Day One

Our labs are equipped with industry-standard tools and equipment. You'll train in environments designed to mirror real-world job sites — not just read about them in a textbook.

Small Class Sizes

Smaller classes mean more one-on-one time with industry-experienced instructors who know the trades inside and out — and who are invested in your success.

Career Services Support

AAI's Career Services team offers resume assistance, interview preparation, and job search support to help connect you with local employers in your trade — even after graduation.*

Flexible Scheduling

Day and evening class options let you train around your existing commitments — whether you're working, parenting, or both. New classes start every 5 weeks on a rolling basis.

Affordable Programs

AAI offers competitive tuition with financial aid options for students who qualify — including federal grants, loans, and military benefits — so cost doesn't have to stand in the way.

Community Focused

Founded in 1968, AAI has been training skilled trades professionals in the Glendale community for over 50 years. We're proud to serve the Phoenix metropolitan area and its employers.
