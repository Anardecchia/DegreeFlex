Technology Programs

Six Paths Into a Tech Career

Every SCI Technology program is 100% online and built around what the industry actually needs — real certifications, practical project work, and the kind of applied skills that stand out on a resume when you're competing for your first tech role.

Computer Support Specialist

Train to troubleshoot hardware, software, and networks — the fundamental skills every IT department needs. Available 100% online and also offered on campus at select locations. SCI prepares you for CompTIA A+ and other entry-level IT certifications that employers recognize nationwide.

CompTIA A+ Prep Online Available Campus + Distance
Cyber Security

Learn to protect networks, systems, and sensitive data from threats, hackers, and unauthorized access. SCI's online Cyber Security program preps you for CompTIA Security+, CISSP, and CEH — three of the most in-demand credentials in a field where Texas employers are actively hiring.

CompTIA Security+ Prep 100% Online CISSP / CEH Pathway
Data Science

Master databases, programming fundamentals, data wrangling, visualization, and statistics — then apply them in a real group capstone project using agile-style daily scrum meetings. SCI pays for your certification within 3 months of graduating. One of the most in-demand skill sets in the modern economy.

SCI Pays Cert Fee Capstone Project 100% Online
Software Developer

Learn to design, write, test, and maintain software — the code that runs applications across every industry. SCI prepares you for Microsoft, Oracle, and CompTIA certifications that validate your skills to employers. The program builds both technical coding ability and the communication skills development teams actually need.

Microsoft / Oracle Certs 100% Online Front + Back-End
UI/UX Designer

A 33-week, design-focused online program covering the full UX process — user research, empathy maps, wireframes, sitemaps, and UI patterns — plus hands-on work in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Learn to build experiences that users actually want to use, using the same tools and methods that design teams use in the real world.

33-Week Program HTML/CSS/JS Included 100% Online
AAS in Computer Science Management

SCI's most advanced tech credential — an Associate of Applied Science degree combining programming, algorithm design, front and back-end development, databases, cloud deployment, AI fundamentals, and machine learning. Built for people who want both technical depth and the management skills to lead tech teams.

Associate Degree AI + Cloud + Dev 100% Online
Why Southern Careers Institute

Built for People Ready to Break Into Tech

SCI's tech programs skip the filler and go straight to the skills that actually matter. Here's what makes SCI different from every other option in Texas for someone trying to launch a tech career without a four-year degree.

100% Online — Complete From Anywhere

Every SCI Technology program is delivered via distance education. No commuting, no rigid campus schedules. Study from home, work around your life, and still get the same accredited credential that on-campus students earn.

Cert Prep Built In — Not Bolted On

SCI programs are designed to prepare you for CompTIA, Microsoft, Oracle, and other industry certifications. For Data Science, SCI even pays your certification fee within 3 months of graduating. The goal isn't just a diploma — it's credentials employers recognize.

Real Project Work, Not Just Theory

From Data Science capstones using agile scrum to UX design labs and software development portfolios, SCI tech programs include applied work you can actually show employers. Not just coursework — deliverables.

Mentors Who Actually Respond

SCI online students consistently mention having a mentor "one message away" when they hit a wall. That's not a chatbot or a ticket system — it's a real person who knows the curriculum and is invested in you getting through it.

No Prior Tech Experience Required

Most SCI tech programs have no coding prerequisites. Students with zero background in tech have successfully completed programs and transitioned into tech careers. SCI will meet you where you are and build from there.

Career Hub + Job Readiness Support

SCI Career Hub connects graduates with actual job postings from employers. You also get achievement badges for top performance — visible credentials that make your profile stand out to hiring managers browsing candidates at the same level.
