WHAT YOU CAN STUDY

CAREER-READY TRAINING PROGRAMS

Each program includes hands-on lab work, experienced instructors, and preparation for industry-recognized certifications. Get trained and get to work.

NURSE AIDE

Build a foundation in patient care with SVTI's state-approved Nurse Assistant Training and Competency Evaluation Program. A direct pathway into healthcare and a stepping stone to advanced nursing careers.

CNA CertificationHealthcare Entry

WELDING & FABRICATION

Learn SMAW, GMAW, GTAW, and FCAW welding techniques in a fully equipped lab. Graduates earn American Welding Society certification — one of the most in-demand trade credentials in the country.

AWS CertificationFabrication Skills

WOOD MANUFACTURING

Pennsylvania has the most abundant hardwood forests in the U.S. This industry-endorsed program trains you in cutting-edge wood manufacturing techniques for a thriving regional economy.

Industry EndorsedRegional Demand
WHERE GRADUATES WORK

CAREERS ACROSS HIGH-DEMAND INDUSTRIES

Central Pennsylvania employers are actively looking for skilled workers. SVTI graduates earn industry certifications that open doors across multiple sectors.

HEALTHCARE FACILITIES

Hospitals, nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and home health agencies across the region need certified nurse aides.

CONSTRUCTION & INFRASTRUCTURE

AWS-certified welders work on bridges, pipelines, buildings, and industrial equipment — with jobs available nationwide.

MANUFACTURING

Automotive, aerospace, and metal fabrication shops rely on skilled welders and fabricators to keep production running.

LUMBER & WOOD PRODUCTS

Pennsylvania's hardwood industry — furniture, cabinetry, flooring, and specialty products — needs trained wood manufacturing professionals.

ADVANCED NURSING CAREERS

CNA experience is often the first step toward LPN, RN, and advanced nursing programs for those who want to grow in healthcare.

SELF-EMPLOYMENT

Welding and wood manufacturing skills translate directly to independent contractor and small business opportunities.
