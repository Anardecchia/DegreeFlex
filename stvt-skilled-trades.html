Skilled Trades Programs

Choose a Program That Moves You Forward

A four-year degree isn't for everyone. STVT offers flexible, affordable training in high-demand trades — programs designed to step away from the desk and into a hands-on career.

HVAC & Basic Refrigeration

Learn the technical skills to repair and maintain HVAC and refrigeration systems. Train to become a hardworking, helpful professional in the HVAC field with hands-on experience on real equipment.

10 Months 50 Credit Hours Diploma
Brownsville · Corpus Christi · San Antonio · McAllen · Arlington
Automotive Service Technician

Passion for cars? Turn it into vital career skills. Train in hands-on labs, learning how to properly run tests and diagnostics and repair systems. Prepare for certifications that may help make you a more desirable employment candidate.

13 Months 71.5 Credit Hours Diploma
San Antonio · Corpus Christi · Arlington · McAllen
Combination Welding

Automotive, manufacturing — welders can find work in a range of industries. Learn how to perform SMAW, GMAW, GTAW, and FCAW welds. Practice on pipe, plate, and grooves with industry-experienced instructors.

10 Months 56.5 Credit Hours Diploma
Corpus Christi · San Antonio · Weslaco
Electrical

Take pride in your work. Train in electrical installation that's safe and up to code, so you can pursue entry-level careers in servicing electrical work across a range of settings — residential and commercial.

10 Months 50 Credit Hours Diploma
Brownsville · McAllen · Arlington
Diesel–Heavy Truck Technician

Spend your days working with heavy-duty engines, keeping rigs running. Learn skills from industry-experienced instructors in topics from mechanical to electrical systems for large-scale equipment.

13 Months 60 Credit Hours Diploma
Arlington · San Antonio
CDL Training: Class A & Class B

Train for a commercial driver's license and quickly get on the road in 1–2 months. Class A covers tractor trailers. Class B covers commercial vehicles. Both prepare you for FMCSA entry-level requirements.*

1–2 Months Certificate FMCSA Prep
Brownsville · McAllen · San Antonio · Corpus Christi · Arlington · Weslaco
