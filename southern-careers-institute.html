Allied Health Programs

Five Paths Into Healthcare

Every SCI Allied Health program is built around what employers actually hire for — clinical skills, administrative knowledge, and national certifications that prove you're ready on day one.

Medical Assistant

Train for both clinical and administrative roles — vitals, EKGs, phlebotomy, patient scheduling, and electronic health records. One of the most in-demand healthcare careers in Texas, with employment projected to grow 14% through 2032.

CCMA Cert Prep Externship Included Clinical + Admin
Pharmacy Technician

Learn pharmacy operations, pharmacology basics, drug dispensing, computer applications, and patient care support. SCI is an authorized certification testing site — you'll be prepped to sit for national exams before you graduate.

ExCPT / PTCE Prep Retail & Hospital Settings Hands-On Lab
Medical Billing & Coding Specialist

Master ICD-10, CPT coding, insurance billing, and electronic health records. Available online — one of the few SCI programs you can complete remotely. Ideal for anyone who wants a healthcare career without clinical duties.

Available Online CBCS Cert Prep Remote-Friendly Career
Medical Office Specialist

Front-office healthcare administration — patient intake, scheduling, medical terminology, billing basics, and health records management. Designed for fast entry into clinic, hospital, and physician office environments.

CMAA Cert Prep Fast-Track Program Office & Clinic
AAS in Healthcare Management

SCI's most advanced allied health credential — an Associate of Applied Science degree combining healthcare operations, business management, and leadership skills for those aiming for supervisory roles in medical settings.

Associate Degree Management Track Leadership Skills

Why Southern Careers Institute

Built for People Who Learn by Doing

SCI's model is built around practical outcomes, not seat time. Here's why thousands of Texas students have chosen SCI since 1960.

Hands-On from Day One

SCI classes go well beyond reading and lecture. You'll train with clinical equipment, practice real procedures in lab settings, and develop the muscle memory employers actually need on the floor.

Finish in Under a Year

Most Allied Health programs at SCI are designed to be completed in under 12 months — so you're not spending years in school before you can start earning. Get in, get certified, get working.

Real Externship Experience

SCI places students in externships at actual clinics, hospitals, and medical offices — not simulations. Graduates enter the workforce with real work experience on their resume, not just a certificate.

Certification Prep Built In

SCI is an NHA Authorized Testing Center. Certification prep for CCMA, CMAA, ExCPT, CBCS, and other national credentials is woven into the curriculum — you don't have to figure that part out on your own.

Employer-Tailored Training

SCI's mission is to be a leading talent producer. Programs are designed with employer input, and SCI Career Hub connects graduates with job postings from healthcare employers actively looking to hire.

A Community That Shows Up

Students consistently describe SCI as a close-knit environment where instructors push you because they want you to succeed — not just to fill seats. The SCI family is a real thing, not a tagline.

Accreditation & Recognition

Your Credential Has Weight

SCI is fully accredited and approved by state and national bodies — which matters when employers are looking at your resume and when you're sitting for certification exams.

Council on Occupational Education (COE)

Institutional accreditation recognized by the U.S. Secretary of Education — the same standard employer hiring teams look for.

Texas Workforce Commission Approved

Approved and regulated by the TWC Career Schools and Colleges division — ensuring programs meet Texas workforce standards.

NHA Authorized Testing Center

SCI campuses are authorized by the National Healthcareer Association to administer certification exams on-site, including CCMA, CMAA, and CBCS.

Texas Veterans Commission Approved

SCI is approved to train eligible veterans. GI Bill® benefits accepted. Military grants available for active duty, veterans, and eligible dependents.
