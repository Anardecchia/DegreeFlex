Welding Programs

Five Paths to a Welding Career

Whether you want to get working in 7 months or earn an associate degree with inspection credentials, TWS has a program built around your goals — with hands-on booth training, flexible scheduling, and real-world prep from day one.

Professional Welding Training

TWS's flagship program — 7 months of hands-on training across SMAW, MIG, TIG, High-Frequency TIG, Flux-Core, Structural, Pipe, Aircraft, and Pipeline welding. You'll train in real welding booths with flexible morning, afternoon, or evening class options. Available in Tulsa, Jacksonville, Houston, and Dallas.

7 Months AWS Cert Prep 4 Campuses Flexible Schedule
Welding & Pipefitting Training

A 9.5-month program that adds structural welding, basic and advanced pipefitting, thin alloy welding, and steamfitting to the core welding foundation. Graduates earn a diploma and are prepared to test for AWS Certified Welder status — ideal for oil & gas, construction, and industrial maintenance careers. Available in Jacksonville and Houston.

9.5 Months Pipefitting Included AWS Cert Prep
AOS in Welding Technology

A 14-month Associate of Occupational Studies degree that takes students through every stage of the welding process — from hands-on fabrication through quality control, inspection procedures, and advanced welding management. Designed for students who want to master welding at a deeper level and open doors to supervisory and technical roles. Available in Tulsa and Jacksonville.

14 Months Associate Degree Quality Control
AAS in Welding Inspection & Quality Management

A 14-month Associate in Applied Science degree combining hands-on welding fabrication with advanced welding inspection and quality management — one of the highest-earning specialty paths in the welding industry. Prepares graduates for welding inspector and quality management roles across aerospace, energy, and industrial sectors. Available in Houston and Dallas.

14 Months Applied Science Degree Inspection Specialty

Why Tulsa Welding School

75 Years of Building Welders Who Get Hired

TWS has been training welders longer than most of its competitors have been in business. Here's what that experience looks like in the classroom — and what it means for you.

Welding Booth Training from Day One

TWS students don't spend their first weeks in lecture halls reading about welding — they're in booths, running beads, and building muscle memory with real equipment. Hands-on instruction is the foundation of every program.

Faster Than Any Four-Year Degree

The fastest TWS program takes just 7 months. The BLS confirms that professional welding training — not a four-year degree — is the standard path into the field. TWS gets you there in a fraction of the time, at a fraction of the cost.

AWS Certified Testing Facility

TWS's Jacksonville campus is an accredited AWS testing facility — which means you can prep and test for the Certified Welder credential without leaving campus. AWS certification is the gold standard in the industry and transferable across all 50 states.

Flexible Class Scheduling

Morning, afternoon, and evening classes are available at TWS campuses — so you can fit training around work, family, or other obligations. You don't have to put your whole life on hold to build a welding career.

Industries That Actually Hire

TWS trains students for industries that have had a constant, documented need for skilled welders: oil & gas, aerospace, shipbuilding, construction, manufacturing, renewable energy, and military. These aren't shrinking fields — AWS projects 320,500 welding job openings by 2029.

Career Services After Graduation

TWS doesn't hand you a diploma and send you off. Career services include interview prep, resume building, and employers coming to campus to recruit. Many graduates are working within weeks of finishing the program.

Accreditation & Approvals

Credentials That Matter to Employers

TWS is recognized by the accreditation bodies and state agencies that employers actually check when they're deciding whether to trust a candidate's training.

ACCSC Accredited

Tulsa Welding School is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) — one of the most recognized accreditors for career and vocational institutions.

AWS Testing Facility

TWS's Jacksonville campus is an AWS Accredited Testing Facility (ATF) — meaning students can sit for the Certified Welder exam on campus, a credential valid and recognized across all 50 states.

Texas Workforce Commission

Texas campuses (Houston and Dallas) are approved and regulated by the Texas Workforce Commission Career Schools and Colleges section — a requirement for operating as a career school in the state.

Title IV Financial Aid Eligible

TWS is authorized under federal law to enroll students who qualify for Title IV federal financial aid programs, including Pell Grants, federal loans, and military education benefits including GI Bill®.
