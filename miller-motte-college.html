Healthcare Programs at Miller-Motte College

Real Training for Real Healthcare Careers

Every Miller-Motte healthcare program is built around practical skill development, industry-recognized credentials, and career placement support. Whether you're drawn to patient care, clinical support, dental, surgical, or the business side of healthcare — Miller-Motte has a focused program designed to get you there.

Dental Assisting
Diploma Program · On-Campus & Online

Train to work alongside dentists in clinical and administrative roles — from chairside procedures and patient care to dental records, scheduling, and front-office operations. One of the most in-demand entry-level healthcare careers.

  • Chairside assisting & dental procedures
  • Radiography and infection control training
  • Dental office administrative skills
  • On-campus & fully online options available
Get Program Info
Medical Clinical Assistant
Diploma Program · On-Campus

Prepare for a versatile clinical and administrative role at the center of patient care. Medical Clinical Assistants are the backbone of physician offices, clinics, and outpatient facilities — taking vitals, assisting with exams, managing records, and keeping practices running.

  • Clinical skills: vitals, injections, EKG, phlebotomy
  • Administrative: scheduling, billing, EHR basics
  • Externship placement with area healthcare employers
  • Industry-recognized certification prep
Get Program Info
Medical Billing & Coding
Diploma Program · On-Campus & Online

Learn to translate medical records into billing codes that drive reimbursement across hospitals, physician offices, and insurance companies. Medical billers and coders work behind the scenes keeping healthcare's financial engine running — and can often work remotely.

  • ICD-10-CM, CPT, and HCPCS coding systems
  • Medical terminology & anatomy for coders
  • Insurance claims & reimbursement processes
  • CPC exam preparation included
Get Program Info
Surgical Technology
Associate Degree · On-Campus

Surgical Technologists are essential members of the operating room team — preparing sterile fields, passing instruments to surgeons, and ensuring patient safety throughout procedures. One of the most specialized and rewarding careers in allied health.

  • Sterile technique & OR protocol training
  • Anatomy, physiology & surgical procedures
  • Clinical rotations in hospital OR settings
  • CST (Certified Surgical Technologist) exam prep
Get Program Info
Healthcare Information Technology
Associate Degree · On-Campus & Online

Sit at the intersection of healthcare and technology — managing electronic health records, health data systems, and the information infrastructure that modern healthcare runs on. A growing field with strong demand for professionals who understand both worlds.

  • Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems training
  • Health data management & information governance
  • Healthcare privacy, compliance & HIPAA
  • RHIT exam preparation pathway

Accreditation & Approvals

Credentials That Healthcare Employers Recognize

Miller-Motte College's accreditations and approvals are what healthcare employers, licensing boards, and certification bodies look for. These aren't just checkboxes — they're the difference between a credential that opens doors and one that doesn't.

ACCSC Accredited

Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges — the national standard for career and technical institutions. ACCSC accreditation signals to employers that Miller-Motte graduates have received quality, career-focused training.

Title IV Financial Aid

Certified by the U.S. Department of Education to participate in Federal Title IV financial aid programs, including Pell Grants and federal student loans. Students may also be eligible for institutional scholarships and VA education benefits.

Certification Exam Prep

Programs are aligned with national certification exams — including the CPC (medical billing), CST (surgical technology), and RHIT (healthcare IT) — so graduates are exam-ready and credential-eligible upon completion.

Military & VA Benefits

Miller-Motte College actively supports military students, veterans, and eligible dependents. VA education benefits including the GI Bill® and military tuition assistance are accepted, with dedicated military resources and advisors at campus locations.
