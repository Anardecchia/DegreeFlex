Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning (HVAC)

Nearly every home, office, and building relies on HVAC systems to stay safe and comfortable — and skilled technicians are essential for keeping them running. At Tidewater Tech's HVAC program, you'll gain hands-on experience with commercial refrigeration, electrical components, and airflow dynamics. You'll develop skills troubleshooting faulty AC units, repairing heating systems, and installing ductwork. Course areas include: Air Conditioning & Refrigeration, Heating Systems, Commercial A/C & Refrigeration, Heat Pumps, Comfort & Psychometrics, and Advanced Troubleshooting. As a full-time student, you could graduate in 48 weeks. BLS projects 9% growth for HVAC mechanics through 2033. Available at the Branch Campus.

Branch Campus 48 Weeks EPA 608 Certification OSHA-10 DPOR Journeyman Prep 9% BLS Growth
Combination Welding

If you enjoy working with your hands, machines, and metals, welding offers opportunities across automobiles, ships, appliances, bridges, buildings, and cutting-edge processes using lasers and ultrasonic waves. Tidewater Tech's Combination Welding program teaches metal fabrication and repair — MIG (GMAW), TIG (GTAW), and Stick (SMAW) welding plus plasma and oxy-fuel cutting, blueprint reading, measurement, and hazard assessment. Train at an AWS Accredited Testing Facility — one of only 4 in Virginia. Graduate in as few as 33 weeks. Certifications available: FCAW 3G/4G, GTAW 2G/3G/4G, SMAW 3G/4G, and AWS Pipe 6G. Available at the Main Campus.

Main Campus 33 Weeks AWS Accredited Facility MIG / TIG / Stick FCAW · GTAW · SMAW
Combination Maritime Welding

Hampton Roads is one of the nation's biggest shipping ports, and certified maritime welders keep those massive ships and barges seaworthy every day. Tidewater Tech's Maritime Welding program teaches welding fundamentals with a focus on the specialized processes used in shipbuilding — including SMAW in 3G/4G on carbon steel plate and 1-inch AASHTO M plate, FCAW 3G/4G, and GTAW in the 6G pipe position. You'll also master blueprint reading, measurement, and hazard assessment. Train at our state-of-the-art AWS Accredited Testing Facility and graduate in 48 weeks ready for shipyards locally and around the world. Additional certifications: OSHA 10-Hour Maritime Shipyard Employment Safety and AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Code. Available at the Main Campus.

Main Campus 48 Weeks AWS D1.1 Cert OSHA Maritime FCAW · GTAW 6G · SMAW
Auto Technician

Tidewater Tech's Auto Technician program provides the skills you need for an entry-level automotive repair career. You'll gain hands-on experience in vehicle maintenance, inspection, diagnosis, and repair — developing expertise in areas like performing pre-alignment system checks, flushing a power steering system, and diagnosing an automatic transmission. Instruction covers: electrical and electronic systems, heating and A/C systems, brake systems, suspension and steering, engine diagnosis and repair, automatic and manual transmissions, and Vehicle Safety Inspection prep. The on-campus auto lab features vehicle lifts, an alignment rack, and modern diagnostic equipment. Graduate in 48 weeks with prep for ASE certification. Available at the Main Campus.

Main Campus 48 Weeks ASE Student Cert EPA Section 609 OSHA-10
Building & Construction Trades

Building and Construction Trades opens doors to careers in new construction, hospitality, apartment complexes, and commercial and residential buildings. You'll learn structural, mechanical, and electrical skills from experienced instructors — including installing electrical wiring and switches, putting up sheetrock, maintaining HVAC systems, tiling, flooring, plumbing, and construction code compliance. As a full-time student, you could graduate in as few as 33 weeks and start working in a field with strong, ongoing demand. Certifications include the EPA Section 608 Refrigerant Transition Certification and OSHA-10. Available at the Branch Campus.
