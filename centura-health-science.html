Health Science Programs

Find the Program That Fits Your Career Goals

Whether you want to work chairside with patients, run a medical office, launch a career in esthetics, or earn a degree — Centura has a health science program built around your goals.

Medical Assistant Diploma

Centura's flagship health science program. Learn clinical skills, administrative tasks, and patient care across physician offices, hospitals, urgent care, and labs. Capstone course preps you for the CMA (AAMA) or RMA (AMT) certification exam. Available at all three campuses.

12 Months Diploma CMA/RMA Prep
Medical Assistant with Limited Scope Radiologic Technology

Everything in the Medical Assistant diploma program, plus the X-ray skills to pursue limited scope radiologic technologist licensure. You'll learn radiation safety, equipment operation, and general imaging procedures — and cap out with two certification prep courses. Available at Chesapeake, Newport News, and Norfolk.

Diploma X-Ray Skills ARRT Prep
Professional Esthetician Diploma

Start your skincare career with Centura's 7-month Professional Esthetician program. You'll build foundational skills in skincare treatments, client communication, and essential esthetics practices — preparing you for licensure and entry-level roles in salons, spas, med spas, and dermatology offices. Available at Chesapeake.

7 Months Diploma Licensure Prep
Master Esthetician Diploma

Take your esthetics career further. The Master Esthetician program builds on foundational training with advanced treatments, leadership skills, and preparation for specialized roles in medical spas, dermatology practices, salons, and wellness centers. Designed for those ready to level up in the industry. Available at Chesapeake.

Diploma Advanced Esthetics Med Spa Ready
Associate of Occupational Science in Medical Assisting

Step up from the diploma to an Associate of Occupational Science degree in Medical Assisting with a Concentration in Clinical Support. A natural pathway for graduates who want to increase their clinical authority and open doors to more advanced healthcare roles. Available at Chesapeake.

Degree Clinical Support Chesapeake
Bachelor of Science – Allied Health Management & Operations

Centura's highest-level health science credential. The BS in Business offers concentrations in Allied Health Management or Operations Management — equipping graduates with leadership, administrative, and business skills applicable across healthcare and beyond. Available at Chesapeake.

Bachelor's Degree Leadership Track Healthcare Management
Why Centura College

Healthcare Training That Gets You Hired

Centura College has been training career-focused students since 1969. Here's what that looks like inside the classroom — and what it means for your career.

Hands-On Clinical Labs from Day One

Centura students don't just read about patient care — they practice it in labs designed to mirror real clinical environments. From vitals and injections to X-ray procedures, you'll train with the equipment you'll actually use on the job.

Diploma in as Little as 12 Months

The Medical Assistant diploma can be completed in as little as 12 months — so you're not spending years in school before you can start earning. That's a real career, without the four-year price tag or timeline.

Certification Exam Prep Built In

The Medical Assistant program culminates with a Capstone course specifically designed to prepare you for the CMA (AAMA) or RMA (AMT) certification exam. Credentials that employers actually look for — baked into the curriculum.

Flexible Day & Evening Classes

Day, afternoon, and evening schedules are available across Centura's Virginia campuses. You don't have to put your life on hold — training should work around your obligations, not the other way around.

Career Services After Graduation

Centura's Career Services Department provides resume writing, interview coaching, and job placement assistance to students, graduates, and alumni. The support doesn't end when you get your diploma.

Military & VA Benefits Accepted

Centura accepts VA education benefits, military tuition assistance, and participates in the Yellow River Program. Active duty, reservists, and veterans are welcome at all campuses — with dedicated staff to help navigate the process.
