Why Southern Technical College

Trades Professionals Train Here. Florida Contractors Hire Them.

Southern Technical College is committed to providing education that prepares students to pursue successful careers and lead enriched lives. Every Technical Trades program is designed with today's trades employer needs in mind — and that means real tools, real equipment, and instructors who've worked the job.

Hands-On Labs with Real Trades Equipment

STC's trades labs are designed to mirror real job-site conditions. Students gain hands-on experience with actual HVAC systems, electrical panels, and welding equipment from day one — not just classroom theory.

Industry Advisory Boards

STC's trades programs have advisory boards made up of representatives from leading Florida construction, electrical, and HVAC companies. These boards shape curriculum to match what employers and contractors are actively looking for when they hire.

ACCSC Accredited

Southern Technical College is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) and approved by the Commission for Independent Education, Florida Department of Education.

Classroom + Job-Site Simulation

STC programs integrate both classroom theory and practical, hands-on lab work. The combination is specifically designed to build the technical knowledge, tool competency, and job-site confidence contractors expect on day one.

Financial Aid Available
